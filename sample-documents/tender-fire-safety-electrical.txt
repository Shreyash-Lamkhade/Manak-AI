CHAPTER 1: FIRE DETECTION AND ALARM SYSTEM (FDAS)

1.1 SCOPE OF WORK

The contractor shall design, supply, install, test, and commission a complete
addressable Fire Detection and Alarm System covering approximately 38,000 sq.m of
the T2 terminal building, including:
- Departure and arrival halls
- Aerobridges (6 nos.)
- Baggage handling area
- Fuel farm control room
- All service corridors and plant rooms

1.2 STANDARDS AND CODES OF COMPLIANCE

The FDAS shall be designed and installed strictly in accordance with:
- IS 2189:2008 — Selection, installation and maintenance of automatic fire detection
  and fire alarm systems (primary reference standard)
- IS 15105:2002 — Design and installation of fixed automatic sprinkler fire
  extinguishing systems
- IS 1646:1997 — Code of practice for fire safety of buildings (general)
- NBC 2016 Part 4 — Fire and Life Safety
- DGCA CAR Section 9 for aerodrome fire safety

1.3 FIRE DETECTION EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATIONS

1.3.1 Addressable Smoke Detectors (Optical)
- Technology: Light scattering photoelectric principle
- Sensitivity: 0.1 to 2.0% obs/m, field-adjustable
- Operating voltage: 17V to 28V DC (loop powered)
- Operating temperature range: -10°C to +55°C
- Ingress protection: IP43 minimum
- Alarm indication: built-in LED, 360° visible
- Quantity required: approximately 2,400 nos.

1.3.2 Addressable Heat Detectors (Rate-of-Rise + Fixed Temperature)
- Fixed temperature: 57°C (Class A1) and 90°C (Class A2)
- Rate-of-rise: 8.5°C per minute
- For use in kitchen, baggage hold, engine test cells
- IP54 rating
- Quantity: approximately 480 nos.

1.3.3 Addressable Manual Call Points (Break Glass)
- Operating voltage: 17-28V DC loop powered
- Reset: requires key or tool
- Colour: red RAL 3000
- Protection: IP54, with break-glass element
- Spacing: not more than 30m travel distance
- Quantity: 340 nos.

1.3.4 Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP)
- Type: Fully addressable, loop-based, EN 54 compatible
- Capacity: minimum 4 loops, expandable to 8 loops
- Each loop: minimum 250 addressable devices
- Display: 10" colour touch screen with floor plan annunciation
- Battery backup: 72 hours standby + 30 minutes alarm
- Battery: sealed lead-acid, maintenance-free
- Output: potential-free contacts for BMS integration
- GSM dialler for remote alarm notification
- Enclosure: IP30 minimum, wall-mounted metallic cabinet

1.4 AUTOMATIC SPRINKLER SYSTEM

Design per IS 15105:2002:
- Hazard classification: Ordinary Hazard Group 2 (OH2) for passenger areas
- Sprinkler spacing: maximum 12 sq.m per sprinkler (OH2)
- Operating pressure at sprinkler head: minimum 0.5 bar
- Sprinkler heads: pendant type, 68°C glass bulb, K-factor 80
- Main supply pipe: MS ERW as per IS 1239 (Part 1):2004 — heavy class
- Branch pipes: CPVC Schedule 40 or MS galvanised
- Flow and pressure test: mandatory hydrotest at 1.5x working pressure for 2 hours
- Water storage tank: 45,000 litres dedicated fire reserve
- Fire pumps: electric main pump + diesel standby pump + jockey pump

1.5 FIRE SUPPRESSION — SPECIAL HAZARD AREAS

For fuel farm, substation, and UPS rooms:
- Clean agent suppression system: FM-200 (HFC-227ea) or NOVEC 1230
- Design concentration: 7% by volume for class C fires
- Discharge time: within 10 seconds
- System design per NFPA 2001 and IS guidelines

2.1 CABLES AND WIRING

2.1.1 Power Cables (LT — up to 1100V)
All LT power cables for general electrical distribution to conform to:
- IS 694:2010 — PVC insulated cables for working voltages up to 1100V
  (for fixed wiring, 2.5 sq mm to 16 sq mm)
- IS 1554 (Part 1):1988 — PVC insulated (heavy duty) electric cables
  (for feeder cables, 25 sq mm and above)

Cable sizing criteria:
- Current carrying capacity as per IS 3961
- Voltage drop not to exceed 3% (lighting circuits) or 5% (power circuits)
- All cable runs in MS conduit or cable trays
- PVC insulation colour code as per IS 732:1989

2.1.2 Fire Survival Cables (Enhanced Fire Performance)
For critical life safety circuits (FDAS, emergency lighting, fire pumps):
- Fire resistant cables: 2-hour fire resistance rating
- Halogen-free low smoke (HFLS) type
- Conforming to IS 17048:2018

2.2 EARTHING

The earthing system shall be designed and installed as per:
- IS 3043:1987 — Code of practice for earthing
- Earthing system: TN-S throughout
- Earth electrode: copper-bonded steel rod, 2.4m length, 14.2mm diameter
- Earth resistance: not exceeding 1 ohm for main MDB earth
- Earth continuity conductor: minimum 50% of phase conductor cross-section

2.3 LIGHTING

2.3.1 Terminal Hall Lighting
- LED luminaires for indoor use: IP40 minimum
- Illuminance: minimum 500 lux (maintained) at working plane
- UGR: less than 19 for occupied areas
- Emergency lighting: minimum 1 lux for 3 hours on mains failure
- Reference: IS 1944 (Part 1) for illuminance recommendations

2.3.2 Apron and Taxiway Lighting
- Flood lighting: LED type, IP65, minimum 100W per fitting
- Pole height: 20 metres
- Reference: IS 10322 (Part 5/Sec 4):2018 for road/area luminaires
