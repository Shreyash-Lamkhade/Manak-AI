B.1 CEMENT

B.1.1 Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC)
All structural concrete shall use Ordinary Portland Cement 53 grade conforming
strictly to IS 269:2015 (Ordinary Portland Cement — Specification).

Technical requirements:
- Grade: OPC 53
- Minimum compressive strength at 28 days: 53 MPa
- Initial setting time: not less than 30 minutes
- Final setting time: not more than 600 minutes
- Fineness (specific surface): minimum 225 m²/kg (Blaine)
- Soundness (Le Chatelier): maximum expansion 10 mm
- Loss on ignition: maximum 4%
- Sulphate content (as SO₃): maximum 3.5%

B.1.2 Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC)
For non-structural work, plastering, and brickwork, PPC conforming to
IS 1489 (Part 1):2015 may be used.
- Flyash content: 15% to 35% by mass of blended cement
- 28-day compressive strength: minimum 33 MPa

B.1.3 Storage and Handling
- Cement bags to be stored in weatherproof godowns on raised wooden platforms
- Maximum stacking height: 10 bags
- FIFO (first in first out) to be strictly followed
- Each consignment to be accompanied by manufacturer's test certificate

B.2 STRUCTURAL STEEL

B.2.1 High Strength Deformed (HSD) Steel Bars — TMT Bars
Reinforcement steel shall conform to IS 1786:2008 (High strength deformed steel bars
and wires for concrete reinforcement).

Grade and properties:
- Grade: Fe 500D (preferred) or Fe 415
- Yield strength (0.2% proof stress): minimum 500 MPa for Fe 500D
- Ultimate tensile strength: minimum 565 MPa for Fe 500D
- Elongation at fracture: minimum 16% for Fe 500D
- Bend test: no crack on bending through 180° over mandrel diameter = 3d (d≤20mm)
- Rebend test: mandatory for Fe 500D
- Carbon equivalent: maximum 0.42% (for weldability)
- BIS certification mark mandatory on each bundle tag

B.2.2 Structural Steel Sections
Rolled steel sections (beams, columns, angles) for steel framework shall conform to
IS 2062:2011 Grade E250 or higher.
Design of steel structures per IS 800:2007 (General construction in steel).

B.3 CONCRETE

B.3.1 Mix Design and Grade Requirements
All structural concrete shall be designed as per IS 10262:2019 (Concrete mix
proportioning — Guidelines) and shall comply with IS 456:2000 (Plain and reinforced
concrete — Code of practice).

Minimum grades:
- Foundations (isolated/combined): M25
- Basement retaining walls: M30
- Columns (ground to 5th floor): M35
- Columns (6th floor and above): M30
- Beams and slabs: M25
- Staircase: M25
- Grade slab: M20 (with steel fibre reinforcement)

B.3.2 Coarse and Fine Aggregates
All aggregates shall conform to IS 383:2016 (Coarse and fine aggregate — Specification).
- Coarse aggregate: 20mm and 10mm graded crushed stone
- Fine aggregate: Zone II river sand or manufactured sand (M-Sand)
- Water absorption of coarse aggregate: maximum 2%
- Flakiness index: maximum 30%
- Los Angeles abrasion value: maximum 35%

B.3.3 Water for Mixing
Potable water conforming to IS 456:2000 provisions.
pH value: between 6 and 8.5.

B.4 MASONRY WORK

B.4.1 Burnt Clay Bricks
Common burnt clay bricks for partition walls and non-load-bearing masonry shall
conform to IS 1077:1992 (Common burnt clay building bricks — Specification).
- Class designation: Class 75 (compressive strength 7.5 MPa minimum)
- Water absorption: not more than 20% by mass (24-hour cold immersion)
- Efflorescence: slight or nil
- Dimensions: 190 x 90 x 90 mm (modular) or 230 x 110 x 75 mm (conventional)

B.5 WATER SUPPLY AND PLUMBING

Internal water supply piping:
- GI pipes (galvanised iron): IS 1239 (Part 1):2004 — medium class
- CPVC pipes for hot water lines: IS 15778:2007
- HDPE pipes for underground supply: IS 4984:2016, PE 100 grade

B.6 ELECTRICAL WORKS (REFERENCE ONLY)

Electrical installation shall conform to:
- IS 732:1989 — Code of practice for electrical wiring installations
- IS 3043:1987 — Code of practice for earthing
- Internal wiring cables: IS 694:2010 — PVC insulated cables 1100V
