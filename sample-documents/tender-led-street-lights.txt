4.1 LED STREET LIGHT LUMINAIRES

The contractor shall supply and install LED street light luminaires conforming to the
following technical specifications for outdoor road lighting on national highways.

4.1.1 Electrical Specifications
- Rated Wattage: 100W (± 5%)
- Input Voltage: 230V AC, single phase
- Frequency: 50 Hz
- Power Factor: minimum 0.95 (lagging)
- Total Harmonic Distortion (THD): less than 10%
- Driver efficiency: minimum 88%
- Surge protection: 10 kV (line to ground), 4 kV (line to line)

4.1.2 Optical / Photometric Specifications
- Minimum luminous flux output: 10,000 lm
- Minimum system efficacy: 100 lm/W
- Colour Temperature (CCT): 5700K ± 500K (cool white)
- Colour Rendering Index (CRI): minimum Ra 70
- Beam angle: Type II-M road lighting distribution
- Maintained illuminance (ME2 class): average 10 lux minimum on carriageway

4.1.3 Mechanical and Protection Specifications
- Ingress Protection Rating: IP65 minimum (dust tight, protected against water jets)
- IK Rating: IK08 minimum (impact resistance)
- Housing material: die-cast aluminium alloy, powder coated
- Finish: dark grey powder coat, minimum 60 microns DFT
- Operating ambient temperature: -10°C to +50°C
- Pole mounting: suitable for 60 mm diameter pole top or side bracket
- Weight: not exceeding 8 kg per luminaire

4.1.4 Lifetime and Reliability
- Rated lifetime: L70B50 > 50,000 hours at 25°C ambient
- LED source: high-power SMD LEDs, binned for colour consistency
- No mercury content — RoHS compliant

4.2 MANDATORY CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

All LED street light luminaires must carry valid BIS certification mark.
The product is covered under Quality Control Order (QCO) issued by DPIIT.
Vendors must submit valid BIS licence number at time of bid submission.

Reference Standards to be complied with:
- IS 10322 (Part 5/Sec 4): Luminaires for road and street lighting
- IS 16102 (Part 1): Self-ballasted LED lamps — safety requirements
- IS 1944 (Part 1): Code of practice for lighting of public thoroughfares

4.3 CIVIL WORKS — POLES AND FOUNDATIONS

4.3.1 Galvanised Steel Poles
- Height: 9 metre (effective mounting height)
- Pole shaft: tapered octagonal, 4 mm wall thickness
- Hot dip galvanised as per IS 2629
- Foundation: M20 grade reinforced concrete as per IS 456:2000
- Anchor bolts: 4 nos., 20 mm dia, 600 mm length, 8.8 grade

4.3.2 Underground Cabling
- Power supply cable: PVC insulated, armoured, 1100V grade
- Cable size: 4-core 16 sq mm for branch circuits
- Reference: IS 694:2010 (PVC insulated cables up to 1100V)
- Conduit: 50mm dia HDPE conduit for cable protection (IS 4984:2016)

4.4 CONTROL SYSTEM

- Dimming control: 0-10V or DALI protocol
- Automatic switching: photocell + time-clock dual control
- Centralised monitoring: SCADA/CMS compatible
- Each luminaire to have individual GPS-tagged monitoring point
